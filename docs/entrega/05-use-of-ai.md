## Where and how each tool was used

- Claude Code (Anthropic's AI coding assistant, including smaller Claude models as helpers): repository setup; searching and checking sources for the local context; drafting documentation and the English submission drafts; and writing most of the code: the trend-analysis pipeline and its tests (`analisis/`), the data files it produces (marked "generated with AI" in their `generado_con` field) and the website code (`web/`). Team members reviewed the results and checked the sources by opening them. The NASA POWER results were reproduced independently, and the MODIS results were re-run from the original AppEEARS files (this check was also done with Claude's help).
- Google Stitch (Google's AI design tool): used by the UI/UX members to design the interface (layout and color palette; not the logo). The design mockups in `diseno/pantallas/` and `diseno/componentes/` (desktop screens for Home, Climate, Farming, Fishing and Alerts, plus login and sign-up) were refined in Stitch and then edited by hand in Figma; `diseno/README.md` says so and warns that they contain sample data. Design workflow, confirmed by Ehileen (UI/UX) on 2026-11-15, for all the screens: (1) the team made a mid-fidelity prototype; (2) the screens were refined in Google Stitch; (3) the team made the final edits by hand in Figma (header, footer and button components, active tabs, section colors, logo and texts). The section icons in those mockups are Google Material Symbols (Apache 2.0), not AI-generated. **No Stitch-generated image is shipped on the website**: on 2026-11-15 we listed every image under `web/` (branch `dev`) and found only the logo (Gemini, see below), the 12 Material Symbols icons and the MODIS Terra satellite image from NASA GIBS (CC0, no AI; `web/img/FUENTES.md`). The website code was written separately, following the design specification (`diseno/ESPECIFICACION.md`). Terms reviewed on 2026-11-14 in the app ("Additional Terms of Service for Stitch", https://stitch.google.com/terms): the user must independently verify all AI-generated suggestions, which we did by reworking the screens by hand in Figma. The additional terms do not address ownership of the outputs; they supplement the Google Terms of Service, under which Google does not claim ownership of generated content.
- AI-generated logo: the "Radar BCS" logo (radar rings, cactus and water drop) was generated with Gemini (Google). It was not made with Google Stitch. On the website it appears in the header of every screen **as `web/assets/logo-radar-bcs-ai-generated.png`, with the "AI-generated" watermark built into the image** (the official submission guide requires a visible watermark on generated images; the separate text label next to the logo was removed on 2026-11-15 to save space, see `docs/FUENTES-Y-IA.md`). The footer of every screen also says "AI-generated logo (Gemini)" and the home footer shows an "AI-generated" label next to its logo. In Figma, the header component also carries the label, and `web/assets/logo-radar-bcs-ai-generated.png` is the logo with the label built in. TODO (2026-11-15, branch ui-ux/logo-cabecera): the header now shows a new icon, `web/assets/logo-radar-bcs-icono.png` (radar, cactus and water drop, also generated with Gemini, confirmed by Ehileen), next to the text "Radar BCS" written in HTML, without the "AI-generated" watermark, and the footers no longer carry the AI line. The team lead must decide whether the watermark comes back before submitting; update this paragraph and the Website line below accordingly.
- Claude Code (UI/UX): used to polish the Figma design files (unified header and footer components, active tabs, colors), export the screen references and icon/logo assets, and write the design specification and its checks (`diseno/`, `web/assets/`).
- Google Stitch: the reference screens (imported into Figma as mockups) came from Stitch; the final Figma screens are the team's own work, partly based on those mockups. Terms of use for the Stitch-generated designs reviewed on 2026-11-14 (see above); no personal data or keys were entered into Stitch.
- ElevenLabs (used): generates the English narration of the demo video from our own script (voice: "Tamsin" from the ElevenLabs voice library, model Eleven v4) (`docs/entrega/08b-video-shotlist.md`). Only the voice is generated; the screen footage is a recording of our own website. The video description says so ("Narration is an AI-generated voice (ElevenLabs)"); there is no on-screen label (see the demo video note below).
