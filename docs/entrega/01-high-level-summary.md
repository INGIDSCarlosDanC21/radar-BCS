**Project name:** Radar BCS (team OptiPaz)

**Summary (50-200 words):**

Radar BCS is a free, mobile-friendly website, written in Spanish, that shows how heat, vegetation, rainfall and groundwater have changed in La Paz, Baja California Sur, Mexico, since 2001, using open NASA data. It addresses the Earth System Trend Detective challenge by answering four questions: what is changing, where, by how much, and whether the change is statistically significant. Each variable has a time series, a map of the zones with the most change, and a trend test (seasonal Mann-Kendall test, plus Sen's slope). A final screen gives residents simple actions to stay safe from heat and to save water. It matters because this kind of information usually stays in technical reports and platforms, far from the people who live with the changes. We show what changed, not why: a trend does not prove its cause.
