## Diapositiva 10 (p10, impreso: número no visible)
TIPS ABOUT PROJECT DEMOS

"Demos" are often one of the first things that Global Judges look at to determine whether a project deserves additional attention.

[Tarjeta 1]
Plan Ahead
Decide early whether to create a video or slide deck, and consider the question, "What story does my team want to tell?"

Delegate
Assign a teammate to lead the Demo creation.

[Tarjeta 2]
Visual Storytelling
There are two formats to submit your Project Demo. Regardless of which one you use, be sure to use strong visuals and ensure readability.

Language
Slide decks and projects must be in English.
Videos can be in your native language with subtitles in English.

[Tarjeta 3]
Considerations
May not include any images, video, audio, or other likeness to persons under 18.

"Recognizable names, voices, and likenesses of persons under the age of 18 should not be included in any submission. In the event any individual 18 years or older is featured in any submission, by submitting such material you represent and warrant that such individual agrees that NASA may use such individual's name and likeness in connection with NASA's use of the material hereunder."

All links must be publicly accessible.

Slide decks must be 7 slides maximum.
Videos must be 30 seconds or less.

## Diapositiva 20 (p20, impreso: Page 24)
BEFORE SUBMITTING FOR JUDGING:

📝 Before Submitting for Judging

● ✅ All team members must be registered and listed on the team's Project Page.
● They all must be registered for the same Local Event (your event!)
● They all must be officially part of your event. Not on the Waitlist.

● 🚫 No new members can be added after submission closes.

● 🧼 Keep submissions free of profanity or inappropriate content.

● 🌐 Use written English for:
   ○ Project submission page
   ○ Final project link
   ○ Demo video or deck
     Note: Videos may be in other languages if English subtitles are included.

## Diapositiva 22 (p22, impreso: Page 26)
HOW TO SUBMIT THE PAGE FOR JUDGING:

DEADLINE   (rojo)

Project submission closes on Sunday, November 15 at 11:59 PM local time, in accordance with the time zone of your Local Event.

You must click the "Submit for Judging" button prior to 11:59 PM local time of your Local Event.

Any projects saved or submitted past this deadline will not be eligible for global judging.

No edit requests or exceptions past this deadline are allowed. Do not edit or re-submit the project after the Project Submission Deadline.

Project timestamp must be prior to the Project Submission Deadline for global judging consideration.

Check your project and demo links / verify they are correct and publicly accessible before the deadline!
